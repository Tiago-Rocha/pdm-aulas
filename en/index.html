How it works

  • Each lesson: 30 to 40 minutes of theory on a mobile topic, then practice in the lesson repository (starter/ with TODOs and tests, solucao/ with the final state).
  • Project: app in groups of 2 to 3 on a public API (main proposal: Tempo Açores, on the IPMA API through the course API), in three sprints with an in-class review (02/11, 18/11, 02/12), freeze on 09/12, defences on 14 and 16/12.
  • Assessment: final group project with an individual mark (PRs and defence) and one or two multiple-choice written tests on the theory, planned for 04/11 (lessons 1 to 19) and 07/12 (lessons 21 to 32), to be confirmed. Weights and rubric in Assessment.
  • What you need in every lesson: laptop with Flutter installed, Android phone with a cable, GitHub account.
