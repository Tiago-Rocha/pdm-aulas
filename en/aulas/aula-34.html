Written test 2

Written test 2: forty multiple-choice questions on the theory component of lessons 21 to 32, on paper, closed book, in 75 minutes. After the test, a commented correction of the questions with the most mistakes.

Closed book
Arrive ten minutes early with the test papers already counted per row. Leave this slide projected while they come in: the rules are on the next one and you read them aloud once, at 16:00, before handing out. Phones switched off and in the backpack, not in the pocket. Laptops closed and pushed away.

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Answer 40 multiple-choice questions on the material from lessons 21 to 32 within the time.
  • Identify, in the correction, the "almost right" options you fell for and why.
  • Know exactly what is missing for Wednesday's freeze and for next week's defence.

Rules

  • On paper, closed book. 40 questions, 4 options each, only one correct. No penalty for a wrong answer: answer all of them.
  • 75 minutes, from 16:05 to 17:20. Anyone who arrives after 16:20 does not come in. Nobody leaves before 16:35.
  • Mark only on the answer grid, in pen, with a clear cross. A question with two crosses counts as zero. To correct, strike through the wrong cross and put your initials next to it.
  • On the desk: pen, student card, water. Nothing else. Phone switched off and inside the backpack, on the floor.
  • Queries about the test paper: arm in the air, the teacher comes to you. The material is not explained, only ambiguous wording.
  • Any attempt to copy voids the test for everyone involved, as stated on the Assessment page.

Only the theory component. There are no questions about Dart syntax or about the code in the lesson repositories; there are questions about what a command or a package does and why.

If someone asks "is X in it?", the answer is always the same: everything that is in the theory slides of lessons 21 to 32, and the "For the tests" slides are the points most likely to come up. Do not confirm or deny specific questions.

How the afternoon goes

  1. 16:00 Reading of the rules, test papers handed out face down. Write your name and number on the answer grid.
  2. 16:05 Start. The time remaining stays projected on this screen, updated every 15 minutes.
  3. 17:05 15-minute warning. If you still have blank questions, now is when you fill them in.
  4. 17:20 End. Pens down, test paper and answer grid handed in together. Nobody leaves the room with the test paper.
  5. 17:20 to 17:30 Break. Phones can come out of the backpack.
  6. 17:30 to 18:00 Commented correction.

Commented correction

  • The teacher marks the answer grids during the break and projects the distribution of answers for the eight to ten questions most often missed.
  • For each one: which was the correct option, which option most people chose, and what made it plausible. It is the difference between knowing the answer and knowing why the others are wrong.
  • Anyone who wants to discuss a specific question speaks at the end, individually, with the test paper in front of them. Formal complaints in writing by Friday.
  • The marks are published on Moodle by Wednesday. This test is worth what is stated in Assessment; the final mark for the course is only settled after the defences.
