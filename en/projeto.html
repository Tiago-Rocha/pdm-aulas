Tempo Açores

A weather and trails app for the Azores, built in groups on the course API: the 5-day forecast for each island with IPMA data, hiking trails, class reports with photos, favourites, and working offline.

Brief on Monday The full brief, with what is required, the extras and how it is assessed, is presented in Monday's lesson, 26/10. Until then, each group creates its repository from the template.

Dates

  • Groups of 2 to 3, formed on 21/10.
  • Three sprints, with a review in class on 02/11, 18/11 and 02/12.
  • Freeze on 09/12; defences on 14 and 16/12.

Game: required, extra or does not count?

  • Required: it has to be in the app for the project to count as complete.
  • Extra: it earns points, but only once everything required is done.
  • Does not count: not part of the project. It may be nice, but it is worth nothing.
